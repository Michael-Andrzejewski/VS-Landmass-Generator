title: Musings of an Old Blacksmith
book: aged-brickred
---
Ah, what wonderful weather. Many years have passed since I forged a blade. Once, long ago, I even forged a blade for Jonas Falx himself.

In the twilight of my life, I muse on what we will become. Will the Rot return? Have we finally defeated it?

I suspect not. The Machines still tick in the depths. Strange storms sweep over the world, leaving echoes of rust and ruin.

We all fade eventually. Jonas, bless his soul, bought us time. But I do not think he bought us salvation. Time comes for us all.

I suppose I must entrust the future to others. I watch my grandchildren play as I write these words.

Will they be strong enough? They have not suffered as I suffered. The world they are born into is harsh, but not desperate.

Perhaps they will see the beauty. As I did. And as so many did not.

I bid thee fare well, to those who read the musings of an old blacksmith. May the forges of your heart shape you more than the forge of the world.
