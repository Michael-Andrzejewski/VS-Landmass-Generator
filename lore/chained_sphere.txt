title: The Sealed Container
book: rotten-green
---
The creature has continued to grow. It is no ordinary being; it absorbs men and their flesh and reshapes it into another part of itself.

It is a demon. We lack the means to kill it, so we have sealed it in one of the containers and had one of the Great Eidolons chain it to the sea floor.

We pray it starves. May no soul ever encounter it again.
