title: Jonas' Island
book: aged-darkgreen
---
In all his years, Jonas worked tirelessly for the good of humanity. He seemed supernatural. A saint. An angel. A mere man, who somehow never ceased to donate his spirit for the good of humanity. Even as it cost him everything.

In all his years, Jonas only ever took one thing for himself.

An island.

It was a place he found early, in the beginning days as he searched through worlds to find ways to escape the Rot. He dreamed of turning this island into a paradise one day, after the Rot had been defeated. Or perhaps a place his descendants could live, if he could not. He anchored it into the world and then moved onto greater matters, but he never forgot about it. Even as the Rot clawed away his inventions, his friends, and his entire world, he held onto his dream. That one day, everything could be made right again and he could live on the island and live a grateful life.

To whoever finds this island... may you honor Jonas' dream.
