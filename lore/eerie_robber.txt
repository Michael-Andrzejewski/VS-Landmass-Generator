Tunnel is through. Came up under the marker just as Voss said we would. The black glass goes deep, deeper than I expected, but the earth below is soft and we made good time.

The pit is full of them. Rows and rows, wrapped in cloth that has mostly rotted away. The smell is bad but not as bad as I feared. They have been down here a long time.

Found three chests in the first layer. One had tools, not worth much. The second had a ring, copper, and some letters no one will buy. The third had a brass gear and a small knife with a bone handle. The knife will fetch a price.

Voss wants to go deeper. He says the coffined dead will have better goods on them. He is probably right. The coffined ones were people of some standing.

I do not like this place. The cloth moves when there is no wind. I told Voss it is the ground settling and he agreed, but he looked at me and I looked at him and neither of us believed it.

There is a sound from further in the pit. Low. Steady. Like breathing but not breathing.
