title: The Joy of the Forge
book: aged-orange
---
The joy of bringing metal to life is one of our greatest arts. Not merely the machines; we have been stopped from producing those, ever since the malaise. But the joy of forging a simple sword, sharpening it, imbuing it with your will. The strike of a hammer against hot metal, the force of one's will against the world world. What could be better!
