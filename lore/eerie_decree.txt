title: The Burial Isle
by: Tobias
book: aged-darkbeige
---
By my order, this island is set apart from the living and given over to the dead.

The workers of The Machine, the miners, the bell crews, and the citizens of the towns that served Jonas Falx now lie in fields and roads where they fell. They number beyond counting. Many bear the marks of the Rot, and I will not have them left among those who survived.

The dead will be carried here by barge and laid to rest on this ground, which is far enough from any settlement that the Rot, if it should spread from them, will threaten no one.

I am founding an order of wardens to keep the island. They will receive the barges, lay the dead in pits where single graves cannot be dug, and mark each pit so that it may be found again. They will keep a record of names where names are known.

No one is to settle this island. No one is to build here except as the wardens require for their work. The dead have earned their rest and the living have earned their safety. Both must be kept.

Let the wardens begin their work. The first barges sail within the week.

Tobias
