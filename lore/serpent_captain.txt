title: The Serpent Route
book: aged-olive
---
The captains of the ocean had once spoken of the sea with reverence. Now, they spoke of the sea with fear.

"I tell you, I've seen it. A serpent with a snout larger than the boat. It snapped up Davies and his entire crew in a single bite, and when it plunged beneath the surface, the waves nearly capsized our ship."

The sea captain gestured at the map. "It appeared here. Exactly where the ships have been going missing. We must re-route. I refuse to sail this route again."
