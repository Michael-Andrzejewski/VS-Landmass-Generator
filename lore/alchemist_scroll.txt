title: A Folded Scroll
book: paper
---
"You pursue a fool's errand!" Jonas roared, his eyes blazing blue. He was surrounded by some kind of glimmering blue shield. "Flux is a byproduct of a greater process. Attempting to create doorways with it is a fool's errand."

A young alchemist stood, wearing black and gold, his eyes glittering with dark determination. "You protect the secrets of your art too closely. At the cost of the world. If you would merely tell us how to create our own food, as you do, we would not need to experiment."

Jonas pressed a hand to his head. "What I do is risky. If done wrong, it can destroy an entire castle and everyone in it."

The young alchemist smirked. "We know. But what does it matter, if all those in the castle are doomed anyways for lack of food?"

Jonas paced anxiously. "It is not merely the destruction of a failed attempt. Every attempt lets something leak through. Every attempt makes the next one harder. It fills the world with a certain malaise, a madness. I will not teach it to you."

The alchemist's lips compressed to a thin line. "Then I will learn it myself."

A flicker of blue energy formed at Jonas' hand. The room suddenly rang with reverberations. A great and terrible light filled the chamber.

And then Jonas stopped. With an expression of utter defeat and sorrow, he let the light die and walked away.

From the floor, the young alchemist looked towards the glowing figure with an expression of pure hatred.
