title: The Old Trader's Musings
book: aged-purpleorange
---
How does one find joy in a doomed world?

I suppose we are all doomed in a way. Death comes for us all, and our loved ones. For many years, I found meaning in my trades. Honoring the lord through my wit and industry. Honoring Jonas through my collection of gears. But now I am old and sickly, and the wealth I accumulated seems small.

I remember when this post was grand. A full harbor, many docks, a boat every hour. Now, it is all run down. We are lucky to get a single boat a month. The oceans have become more dangerous.

I will pass the business, and this small trading post, to my son. He is young and industrious. I hope he finds meaning, not only in the collection of wealth, but in the value created from trade and the deeper irrational relationships a simple rational trade can create.
