title: Beware the Serpents
by: Tobias
book: aged-brickred
---
Seraph,

The world was torn asunder after Jonas’ final act with The Machine. So much flesh and machine sank into the depths of the ocean. So much malaise pooled down there.

In the depths of the ocean, that flesh and machine was possessed once more by malaise. There are deadly serpents now that strike at anything man-shaped. I have heard reports of vast serpents and other strange horrors beneath the water, but I have only observed the serpents for myself.

The only reliable solution to avoid them is a boat. But a simple raft will do if one is brave enough.

There is a forested trading island to your East.

Construct a boat or raft and sail over. You should find additional guidance there.

Beware the serpents,
-Tobias
