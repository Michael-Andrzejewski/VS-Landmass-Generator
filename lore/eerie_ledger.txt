title: Barge Ledger
book: aged-gray
---
Day 4. Barge 1. From the lower mill towns. Sixty-three dead. Pit One.

Day 4. Barge 2. From the eastern shaft works. Forty-one dead, many burned. Pit One.

Day 7. Barge 3. From the foundry yards. Eighty dead. Some still in work harnesses. Pit One.

Day 7. Barge 4. From the bell station at Gannow Deep. Thirty-one dead. All divers. Pit Two.

Day 12. Barge 5. From the flux stores. Twenty-nine dead. Badly rotted. We coffined what we could. Pit Two.

Day 12. Barge 6. From the northern road. One hundred and nine dead. Civilians, most of them. Pit Three.

Day 18. Barges 7 and 8. From the Machine grounds. Two hundred and six dead. The loading crews, the gaunt men who carried the tanks. They are the worst to look at. The Great Pit.

Day 23. Barge 9. From downriver. Forty-four. Pit Three. We are running out of cloth.

Day 30. Barges 10 through 12. I have stopped counting the dead. I know their faces now, from the towns I used to trade in. I told Aldric I need a day. He said there are no days. He is right. The barges do not stop.
