title: A Dream of Paradise
book: aged-darkgreen
---
Jonas always dreamed of paradise. In searching through the countless worlds, he became enamored with one. An island where he could raise a home.

It was this dream, this vision of the island, that kept him going. As his world disintegrated into ruin, he kept himself focused on this island. Despite the horrors he encountered as he looked into the various temporal realms, this island kept him going. He never broke.

Because he had a dream.
