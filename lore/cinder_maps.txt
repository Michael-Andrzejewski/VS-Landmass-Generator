title: The Clockmaker's Maps
book: aged-darkbeige
---
Cinder stood, examining a map he had found in a clockmaker’s chest.

“The Archives is a foolish endeavor,” Cinder spoke, his voice cold. The alchemist had a burning glare in his eyes. “All resources must go to The Machine.”

“Sire,” the clockmaker said. “We must have contingencies. The great nations are falling. Even if Jonas succeeds, we must have some way to rebuild. Some way to teach--”

“We will teach nothing.” Cinder spat. He tore up the map. “This knowledge is cursed. Better for it to be destroyed with us.”

The clockmaker lowered their head.

The next day, secretly, they scribbled more maps to the Resonance Archives and began passing them to the seafarers and traders. If Jonas should fail and merely delay the Rot rather than eradicate it, then perhaps some future inventor could learn from the Archives.
