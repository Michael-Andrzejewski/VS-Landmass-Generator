title: The Keeper's Log
book: aged-darkgray
---
I am alone.

I heard a great rumbling and a flash of light split the sky. I glimpsed stars, vast worlds stretching away from me, and gears that dwarfed even the greatest castles. Then I was here, alone in the lighthouse in a vast ocean.

...

It is the third day. I have kept the lighthouse running. A man in a ship approached me. I accepted his food and water, but I declined his offer to explore. I must keep the beacon shining. It seems we have been scattered, but I know not whether the Great Work succeeded. In case it did not, I must retain my post.

...

It is the 64th day. A great storm of rust and fury swept over the world. Many sailors have arrived here, drawn by the beacon of the lighthouse. I've provided them with the maps I've received from others, directing them to small villages and to the remnants of the old world. Although my food is beginning to run low, I must hold my post. There are still sailors lost at sea, looking for the light, and only I can guide them.
