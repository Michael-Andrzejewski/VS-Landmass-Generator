title: A Garden in a Doomed World
book: aged-darkgreen
---
I was once an assistant to a great emperor. When I was stricken with the Rot, I was sent alone with my stricken brothers to die on an island.

But now, the Rot is gone from me. Me and my brothers are alone on this jungle island. There is food and water.

A great storm passed over the world last week. Strange creatures emerged; we barely made it inside in time. The souls of the damned roamed the island for nearly an entire day before the event abated.

And then, it passed.

Jonas must have failed. The Rot must be present somewhere, perhaps far away. One day it will consume us all.

I meditated on this thought. I allowed myself to feel all the despair and fear and anger that came from the knowledge that Jonas had failed.

And then, I felt peace. It is not in us to know our place and our end. Yet the end comes for all of us. Even had Jonas succeeded, my brothers and I would still pass on. Whether we live simply until the next storm, or an entire lifetime on this island, only we alone can make it valuable.

I will turn this island into a paradise. A garden. A tribute. Not merely to help any wandering soul, but for myself, for the sole purpose of bringing beauty to the world and the lives of my brothers and I.

For what higher meaning is there to the creation of beauty in a doomed world?
