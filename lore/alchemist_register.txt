title: Register of the Collegium
book: aged-purpleorange
---
The Collegium of the Alchemical Arts, entered in its own hand.

Founded on the island in the first year of the Concord by six Magisters who wanted somewhere quiet to work, far from the courts and nearer the sea. They built the first quad of the stone they quarried from the hill, and the observatory on the hill to watch the weather of the sky, which they held to be the same weather as the sea's, only slower.

The Collegium keeps nine quads. In them are the libraries, the laboratories where matter is taught to change its mind, the lecture halls, the engine halls, the cabinets of specimens, the refectory and the dormitories of the students. In the great quad stands the clock, which keeps the Collegium's time and, Magister Hale insists, a little of the world's.

The Collegium teaches four arts: the transmutation of metals, the distillation of essences, the reckoning of the heavens, and the folding of distances. The fourth is the youngest and the most argued about.

In the fortieth year the Magisters of the fourth art began the gateway in the bowl on the east shore. Its purpose, entered here at their request: to step from one shore to another without the sea between, and in time from one world to another.

In the fiftieth year the gateway was finished, and its first trial was entered for the summer.

That is the last entry in the Magisters' hands. The next is in a student's: the gate is open, the Devastation is coming out of it, and we are told to leave the register in the library and go.
