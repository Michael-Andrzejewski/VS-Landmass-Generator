title: The Gatekeeper's Log, Last Pages
book: rotten-gray
---
Fourth day of the trial. The ring held its charge through the night. Both coil towers sang the same note by morning, which Magister Orrin says means the field is even round the rim. I logged the gauges every hour. Nothing moved in the ring but the air, and the air shimmered like the air over a forge.

Fifth day. Orrin wants to open it fully. The Collegium voted, eleven to four. I was one of the four. I keep the gate, so I will keep it whatever they vote, but I have written my objection here so that it is somewhere.

Sixth day. We opened it at noon. For a moment the ring was full of another sky, a brown one, with something very large turning slowly in it, like a wheel. Then the light in the ring went out and the shimmer went black, and the black spilled over the rim like water over a weir.

It is not water. Where it touches the stone the stone goes dark and hard and wrong. The grass round the dais is gone. Something like grass is growing back in its place, and it is red.

Seventh day. We cannot close it. The levers move but the ring does not answer them. Orrin went down to the dais to look and came back with his hands black to the wrist. He says it does not hurt.

Ninth day. It has reached the road. The students are leaving by the south gate in every boat we have. The Magisters are staying to work on the ring. I am staying because I keep the gate.

Twelfth day. The library is the last of the halls it has not reached. I carried the register there myself. If anyone finds it, the register says who we were. This log says what we did.

There is no thirteenth day written. The ink on the last page has gone the same dark colour as the stone.
