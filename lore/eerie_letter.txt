title: Unsent Letter
by: Edric
book: aged-orangebrown
---
Dear Mother,

I am sorry I have not written sooner. They keep us at work from before light until well past dark, and when I do sit down I fall asleep where I am. Harmon laughed at me last week because I slept standing against the wall of the loading bay with my hands still on the tank straps.

The work is hard. We carry the flux tanks on our backs up the ramp to the loading cradle. The tanks are heavy and the flux inside them is blue and cold, and sometimes it leaks through the seals and stains our shirts. My hands have gone numb in the mornings. The Clockmakers say that is normal and will pass.

They tell us tomorrow is the last charge. Master Falx himself came through the loading bay today and looked at us. He looked tired. He looked like he had not slept in a very long time. He said something to the foreman I could not hear, and left.

After tomorrow I will be free to come home. I have saved most of my wages and I am bringing you the brass gear I wrote about. Harmon says they are good luck but I think you will like it just for being pretty.

I will see you soon.

Your son, Edric
