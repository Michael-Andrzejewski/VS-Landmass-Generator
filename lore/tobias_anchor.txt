title: Seraph
by: Tobias
book: aged-brickred
---
Seraph,

I have placed the anchor here according to Jonas' instructions, and left his sealed letter in the chest above. I write now with practical matters. In case my village should fail and be consumed by the Rot, I have ordered this island quarantined and erased from all maps. If you should fall into dire straits, there will be no help coming.

It has been many decades since Jonas activated The Machine for the last time. I have been unable to locate it after the shifting of the world. Following the designs from Jonas' original invention, I have been creating a second, larger, anchor to puncture the firmament. I do not know the timeframe this anchor operates on, but I hope that either the anchor on this island, or the anchor from my Tower will draw you back into the world.

This island is rich in copper. To the northeast is an island with tin, and to the the northwest, a trading hub you can visit.

We have abandoned the tin mine due to an increased malaise and monstrous presence. We hope to reclaim it soon, but if not, you must prepare for a difficult fight.

The oceans are filled with horrors. Sail carefully, and do not swim.

Find the mine, and find the trading hub. When you reach me, I will be waiting.
-Tobias
