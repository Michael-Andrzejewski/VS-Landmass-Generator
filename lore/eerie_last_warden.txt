title: Last Entry
book: aged-cherryred
---
I am the last. Brother Collum took the boat three weeks ago and did not come back. I do not blame him. The others left before that, one by one, until it was just us two, and then just me.

The drifters come at night now. I can see them from the gate, moving across the downs in their slow way, drawn to something here. Perhaps the pits. Perhaps the Rot. I bar the door and wait for morning. They have not tried the walls yet.

The Rot is at the eastern shore. A dark stain on the rocks that was not there in spring. It creeps. I have watched it these past weeks and it is wider each time I look. I do not know if it comes from the water or from the dead below.

I could leave. The boat is sound and the other isles are not far. But someone must tend the markers and renew the ochre. Someone must know where the dead are laid. If the last warden leaves, then all these people become nothing, just bones in the earth with no one to say they were here.

So I stay. I will renew the crosses in the morning, and I will keep the record, and when the candles run low I will sit in the dark and listen to the sea.
