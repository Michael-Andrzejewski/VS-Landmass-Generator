title: The Scribe's Record
book: aged-darkbeige
---
"What have I done?" Jonas whispered.

Beside him, the scribe wrote down every word. Jonas stood on the ship, looking towards a vast heap of devastated garbage.

The area was filled with devastated chunks of material.

As Jonas watched, a skeletal figure scampered across the metal, collecting precious metals and delicate gears from the heaps of assorted material.

"What is that?" Jonas asked, his face pale.

"One of the condemned, sir." the captain spoke. "Those who have committed particularly egregious crimes are sent here to earn their freedom. The malaise changes them."

Another figure skittered forward. Huge bone spikes jutted from their shoulders. Rusty metal appeared to have fused with their body. The figure approached, then struck at the skeletal man. The man collapsed. The creature stood over the body and began carefully picking out the ruins. The creature glanced towards the boat and hissed, dozens of rusty wires and bone spikes sticking out. Jonas stumbled away to the edge of the ship and threw up.

The captain watched the scene coldly, staring back at the creature.

"It is simply the nature of the malaise." the captain said. "We require the valuables from this region. It is one of the primary sources of gears and materials that can handle flux."
