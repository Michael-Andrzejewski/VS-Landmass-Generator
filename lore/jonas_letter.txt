title: To Seraph
by: Jonas Falx
book: aged-darkbeige
---
You are reading the ramblings of a dying old man.

I have failed. I do not even know whether it is possible to succeed. I have witnessed horrors that should have remained beyond us. Entire worlds, far greater than ours, turned to Rust.

The Rot bleeds between worlds. There is no escaping it.

Even if the will to do so persisted, I do not believe the remnants of our civilization could rebuild The Machine. I have scattered humanity across the cosmos to buy as much time as possible before we are consumed.

Seraph. You were once a valuable assistant to me. You have been changed by The Machine; how, I don't know. You have likely lost your memories but retained your mind.

I instructed for an anchor to be placed on this island. The time we bought is yours to spend. The world will have shifted from what you once knew, but the remnants are still there.

Perhaps you can succeed where I failed. The Machine is out there somewhere. The Rot will be creeping in at the edges of the world.

Forgive me, Seraph. For all that I have done, and all that I have failed to do,
-Jonas Falx
