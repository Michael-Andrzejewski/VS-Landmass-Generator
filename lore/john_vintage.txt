title: Tally-ho!
by: John Vintage
book: aged-cherryred
---
Tally-ho! I found the source of the malaise. There is a large eidolon, perhaps twice the height of a man. I was able to destroy its legs and trap it in the depths, but the creature nonetheless dealt me a grievous blow.

I may soon pass.

I entrust the work to whoever reads this message. Know that I lived well and fought bravely. May my sacrifice be no more worthy or unworthy that the countless souls who have perished before me.
-John Vintage
