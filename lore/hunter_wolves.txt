title: The Hunter and the Wolves
book: aged-darkolive
---
The hunter crouched in the bushes, eyes narrowed. Silently, he nocked an arrow. The doe was standing in the clearing, unaware of his presence. It was thin, but there was no trace of the Rot upon it.

Food for a week, maybe more. the hunter thought, slowly beginning to draw back the arrow.

Without warning, two wolves crashed through the undergrowth nearby, rushing into the clearing. Savage growls filled the air. The wolves ripped apart the doe as it tried to flee and tore into it. Blood filled the air. The hunter shrank back, watching in horror as the two wolves devoured the deer in moments.

The display of such violence was shocking.

The hunter had grown up near the wilderness. He’d hunted in these woods for many years before the Rot began to spread. And he had always admired the wolves. Their nobility, their teamwork, their determination.

But as he watched the two dark creatures mangle the deer in front of him, he saw only madness. Frothing mouths and maddened yellow eyes. It was as if the same malaise that corrupted the machines had possessed the wolves.

The wolves finished. The corpse was destroyed, strewn across the clearing. Much of the meat remained. These wolves attacked not out of hunger, but out of savagery.

The wolves stalked away.

Dread fell over the hunter. He watched for several minutes until flies began to buzz over the clearing. Then, hunger took the better of him and he crept into the clearing, scraping all the meat he could get off the ground and into a satchel.

If he cooked it, his family could hopefully live another day.
